Privacy Policy

Krool World website · Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers the website at krool.github.io ("the Site"), run by Krool ("we", "us"). The Site is a list of games and tools with links out to them. It has no accounts, no forms, and no advertising.

Analytics

The home page uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages get viewed. It loads with the page and sets a first-party cookie that tells one browser from another. It records anonymous usage data such as the page viewed, the referring site, and the browser and device type. We do not send it your name, email, or anything you type. This privacy page does not load analytics.

To opt out, block cookies for this site or use Google's opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Stored on your device

The Site saves three display settings in your browser's local storage: light or dark theme, category or timeline view, and timeline sort order. They stay on your device and are never uploaded. Clearing site data removes them.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site. GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page, as any web host does.
  • Google Analytics, as described above.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google.

Google's handling is governed by its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. GitHub's is at docs.github.com.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, or phone number.
  • No precise location.
  • No advertising or ad tracking.
  • We do not sell data or share it with anyone beyond the services listed here.
